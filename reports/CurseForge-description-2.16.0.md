# Forever Goal Tracker

Choose long-term goals in **Warcraft Forever**, follow step-by-step guides, and track progress automatically across your characters. Also supports Classic Era.

## Features

- **Gear Planner:** plan a named gear set slot by slot on your character, with item search, filters, a level range, enchants and where each item comes from. Add a set to My Goals to track it, link items to Library goals like Thunderfury, or plan for another character on your roster. A rotatable 3D preview shows your set on a backdrop for your race, with soft weather, and Z sheathes the weapons.
- **169 goals:** weapons, mounts, reputations, raids, attunements, dungeon keys, armor sets, professions, PvP and more.
- **Automatic progress:** tracks levels, items, quests, reputation, skills, gold, mounts and other milestones across your characters.
- **Goal Library filters:** search, filter for your character or any class, faction and category, sort by Progression to see armor sets in the order you'd earn them, and add everything listed at once.
- **Goal suggestions:** find goals suited to your character and interests.
- **Make it yours:** choose individual classes or professions, favorite goals, add notes and adjust supported targets.
- **Helpful links:** item tooltips, quest status, NPC locations, map pins, a map view and Wowhead links. Ctrl-click to preview gear or mounts, or an armor set's icon to try on the whole set.
- **Map view:** a pin button on a goal page fades the banner to the zone map, with a gold pin on the person your next step needs. Zoom with the mouse wheel or the slider. Steps, Exalted reputations and the Onyxia and Zul'Gurub raids have **Show on map** links.
- **Materials panel:** a drawer beside each goal lists what it still needs, counted across your characters. Item tooltips also show which tracked goals need an item.
- **Pop-out steps:** pop a goal's steps out into their own resizable window and keep them on screen while you play. The goals list also folds to a slim icon rail.
- **Clear guides:** search My Goals, hide completed goals and steps, see raid lockouts and follow prerequisite locks on Classic Era.
- **Living goal images:** soft light, sunbeams, embers, snow, blown sand, star glints, a gentle bloom on flames, gems and eyes, and windy leaves on nature banners. Choose Full, Subtle or Off in Settings.
- **Goal artwork:** matching banners with artist credits, for your class, race and faction.
- **Social goals:** guildmates, trades, buffs, campfires and emotes, counted across your characters (on Forever).
- **Forever support:** NEW and UPDATED markers highlight Forever content; guides awaiting verification are clearly marked.
- **Customizable UI:** adjust size, opacity, sounds and celebrations in Settings.

## Getting started

Type `/goals` (or `/fgt`) to open the tracker. Choose **Help me get started** for suggestions, or add goals from the **Goal Library**. Follow them on **My Goals**; steps tick automatically or can be checked by hand. Open the **Gear Planner** tab to plan a set.

Use `/goals settings` for options and `/goals reset` to reset the window. A key binding is available under **Options > Keybindings > AddOns**.
