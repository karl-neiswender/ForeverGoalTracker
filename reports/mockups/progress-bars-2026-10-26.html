A. Slim masthead, quiet bar

Title, gear and close in a 32 px strip, then a 22 px bar. At rest it shows no words. Hover it, or change your progress, and the percentage and goal count fade in inside the bar for a few seconds. Keep the pointer there and the breakdown appears below it. Each bar uses the ticks that are true for it: 25, 50 and 75 here, since this is an average; one per step on goal bars (25, 50 and 75 again past 12 steps); none on the small My Goals card bar. The only setting is ticks on or off.

Reputation goals: one segment per standing

Each segment is one gate, the same width whatever it costs in points, so the bar moves at the pace players think in. Only the stage you're in fills partway. Hover a segment for its numbers. Factions that start below Neutral get a lead-in segment up to Neutral, with its own stages listed in the hover. Drag the reputation slider; the main slider doesn't drive these.
