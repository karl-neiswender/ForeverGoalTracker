Progress bars, second pass

Changes from the first pass: one light per bar (the glint now flows into the tip and makes it flare), a bright trail showing what you just earned, ticks that spark as you pass them, goal bars split into one segment per step, completed segments settled into a quieter gold (green now only means the whole goal is done), the game's own fonts, and the step list and bar lighting each other up on hover.

Main bar

The bar sits centered in its band, with equal space above and below. Hovering lights the rim, which is the hint that there's more to see. Numbers appear inside, and a short summary follows after a pause. When progress goes up, the new stretch flashes and fades into the fill, and any 25/50/75 tick you cross sparks once.

Goal page: one segment per step

The same rule now covers every goal: one segment per step, filled partway when the step has a count (skill, levels, reputation, items). Hover a segment for its step, or hover a step row to light its segment. Finished segments settle into a quieter gold, so the eye goes to the stage you're in. Past 12 steps the bar would become a comb, so it falls back to one continuous bar with 25/50/75 ticks. Out of order: segments always stay in the step list's position, so a later step can fill before an earlier one, leaving a gap. The glowing tip follows the step you last made progress on, not the leftmost one. Steps you can't do yet (the addon's existing requirement locks) are hatched. Try "Progress a later step". Tooltips never point anywhere ("above", "left"): they name the steps instead.

Reputation, as a goal like any other

Each segment is a standing, like the reputation bar on the character sheet: you are in Friendly, so the Friendly segment is filling. No words at rest: hover a segment and one tooltip names that standing, its points, and what it unlocks. Filling the last segment, Revered, is reaching Exalted, and the whole bar turns green. Timbermaw starts at Hostile, so it simply has six segments; a Neutral-start faction has four. Hover any segment for its numbers.

Several things moving at once

Ambassador: Alliance Capitals is four reputations climbing side by side. No new visual language: each segment fills partway on its own, the step list gives every moving step a thin bar of its own, and the one glowing tip sits on whichever you last advanced. One action that moves two steps flashes both trails; the tip goes to the one that moved most. A segment never splits into a bar inside a bar: Stormwind's four standings are one segment, filled a quarter per standing, with the details in its tooltip.
