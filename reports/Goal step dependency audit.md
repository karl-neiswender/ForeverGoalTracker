# Goal step dependency audit

Research date: **October 8, 2026**, before verification of the incoming Warcraft Forever beta build. The audit classifies every current goal address: **79 goals, 1,177 raw addresses and 1,105 counted tasks**. Most rows should remain available: **825 raw rows have no recommended hard lock, 147 have source-supported Classic dependency candidates, and 205 have withheld decisions**. The remaining difference between raw rows and counted tasks is 72 Tier 3 parent pieces, whose material rows carry progress instead. Quest chains provide useful one-to-many unlocks and all-of/any-of prerequisites, while collecting materials, equipment and cumulative milestones generally remains parallel. Several current guides need corrections before locks can accurately represent them. This is a complete classification ledger, not a claim that every row was individually tested or that Classic behavior is confirmed in the incoming Forever build; no production dependencies were implemented.

The [exhaustive JSON ledger](Goal%20step%20dependency%20audit.json) and [CSV ledger](Goal%20step%20dependency%20audit.csv) preserve every current row's exact Forever text, original address/key, counted status, recommendation, all-of/any-of row references, character scope, confidence, source URLs, reasoning and provenance. Nine racial-riding text variants preserve both Era and Forever wording. The [validation result](Goal%20step%20dependency%20audit.validation.json) confirms **zero missing, duplicate, unowned or mismatched rows; zero dangling references or row-reference cycles; and 256 resolved row references**. **No ledger edge is production-ready by default.** `requiresAll` denotes AND over identified row-completion candidates; `requiresAny` denotes OR. Alternative conditions such as native race belong in the accompanying predicate fields. Quest acceptance, quest completion, current item possession, level, reputation, class and instance-state requirements remain separate from row ticks in `externalPredicateNotes`, `alternativeConditions`, `missingPrerequisite` and narrative notes. These fields describe research conditions, not an executable complete predicate language. Empty row-reference arrays do not mean an action lacks gameplay prerequisites. Detailed [quest notes](../research_notes/Goal%20step%20dependency%20audit/quest_dependencies.md), [parallel-goal notes](../research_notes/Goal%20step%20dependency%20audit/parallel_dependencies.md) and [raid/set notes](../research_notes/Goal%20step%20dependency%20audit/raid_set_dependencies.md) retain the source reasoning, limitations and proposed external requirements.

The primary Classic quest baseline is the pinned [Questie v10.0.0 database](https://github.com/Questie/Questie/blob/v10.0.0/Database/Classic/classicQuestDB.lua) and [its corrections](https://github.com/Questie/Questie/blob/v10.0.0/Database/Corrections/classicQuestFixes.lua). Questie group prerequisites mean all required predecessors, single alternatives mean an allowed predecessor, and parent-quest relationships can mean **in progress**, rather than completed. These distinctions matter more than the order of rows in a guide. Independent rows share validated collection/milestone templates; unresolved conditions are explicitly retained rather than turned into inferred hard locks.

## Quest branches provide useful unlocks without forcing linear order

**Lok'delar gives the clearest first fan-out/fan-in pattern.** Its second row starts the demon task; Artorius, Klinfran, Solenor and Simone can then be tackled in parallel. Turning in the four heads requires all four, rather than whichever demon happens to precede the final row. The associated Rhok'delar string quest opens from the Ancient Leaf handoff and can proceed alongside those demons; the bow's final assembly requires the finished stave AND the enchanted string. This cross-goal prerequisite must work even when the player never adds Lok'delar to My Goals. ([Stave of the Ancients](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7636), [A Proper String](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7635))

**Horde Onyxia is another real one-to-many unlock.** Completing Emberstrife unlocks Scryer, Somnus and Chronalis together; Axtroz follows all three. The Alliance chain is largely sequential but compresses several True Masters quests into one row, so the evidence must correspond to its final handoff. A three-way unlock should animate each newly available row, without creating dependencies between siblings. ([Emberstrife](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=6570), [Scryer](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=6582), [Somnus](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=6583), [Chronalis](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=6584), [Axtroz](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=6585), [The True Masters final handoff](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=4224))

**Thunderfury needs an OR start and an AND finish.** Either binding starts the vessel route; both bindings are not required at that point. The summoning turn-in requires both bindings, ten Elementium Bars and the Essence. Arcanite Bars, Fiery Cores and Elemental Flux are ingredients used to make the bars, so buying finished bars bypasses that preparation. The Essence has a quest-state gate, which is more precise than a historical tick on a row labelled “Start.” ([Examine the Vessel](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7785), [Thunderaan the Windseeker](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7786), [Elementium Bar](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17771))

Benediction makes a contained initial prototype: the Eye of Divinity opens the Eris event, the event yields the Splinter, and assembly requires all three components. The tradeable Eye of Shadow stays available throughout. Sulfuras likewise needs an explicit alternate path: a purchased Sulfuron Hammer bypasses personally obtaining plans, supplying crafting materials and having a crafter perform that work. An all-of gate over every earlier row would incorrectly reject that path. ([The Balance of Light and Shadow](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7622), [Eye of Shadow](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=18665), [Benediction](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=18608), [Sulfuron Hammer plans](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=18592))

Dungeon Set 2 supplies the largest repeated fan-out pattern: wrists first; belt and hands together; feet, legs and shoulders together; head and chest together. There is no lock between rewards from the same stage, and full Dungeon Set 1 completion is unnecessary to begin. Matching Dungeon Set 1 pieces are consumed by the appropriate turn-ins. **Use completed stage quests on the same class/faction character as the gate**, rather than trusting that an earlier reward is still owned or that another character ticked its row. The long intervening quests are absent from these collection rows; exact class/faction variant data remains a follow-up before executable quest gates. ([Dungeon set upgrade guide](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/dungeon-sets-1-2-quests-wow-classic), [Hunter wrists example](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=8906/an-earnest-proposition), [Hunter belt/hands example](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=8931/just-compensation), [Hunter three-piece example](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=8952/anthions-parting-words), [Hunter final pair example](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=9000/saving-the-best-for-last))

## Collection and raid history should rarely become hard locks

Reputation tiers, PvP rank milestones, honorable kills, duel wins, money, friends, professions and class levels describe progress toward an outcome. They do not make the next increment of work unavailable. **Keep every ascending milestone available**, including duplicate thresholds created by low adjustable targets. Capital reputations, professions, selected class parts and independent material gathering also remain parallel. Ordinary Tier 1, Tier 2, Dungeon Set 1 and Viper collection rows have no sibling-piece prerequisites; owning a chest does not require first owning shoulders. The current builders and progress rules establish this inventory/goal interpretation. ([Local goal definitions](../Library.lua), [local original/group goals](../Data.lua), [local progress/rule implementation](../Core.lua))

Raid locks need a different kind of evidence. A player can join an instance after earlier bosses died, without a personal completion tick for them. Molten Core and Blackwing Lair attunements are shortcuts, so their checkbox must not universally block raid kills; group keys similarly do not mean each player must own the key. Majordomo has a Classic first-eight-boss/rune condition, but ticks alone omit doused runes and present instance state. Naxxramas's four wings remain independent; their endpoints converge on Sapphiron, then Kel'Thuzad. Treat these as conditional raid-state candidates rather than permanent personal-history locks. ([Attunements and keys](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/classic-wow-attunements-and-keys-dungeons-raids), [Majordomo encounter requirements](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/majordomo-executus-molten-core-strategy-wow-classic), [Sapphiron](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=15989/sapphiron))

**Optional bosses must not gate the final encounter.** Hakkar can be fought with priests alive, though that changes his abilities; the usual full priest clear is a strategy choice. AQ40's Bug Trio, Viscidus and Ouro are optional for the main route. AQ20's Moam, Buru and Ayamiss must not be placed in a mandatory sequence before Ossirian. BWL drake ordering and several physical wing/access gates have not been established strongly enough in this pass to assert hard edges. Source material based on comments or conventional route descriptions has lower authority than an actual quest/door/event record. ([Hakkar](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=14834/hakkar-the-soulflayer), [AQ40 overview](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/zone=3428/ahnqiraj), [AQ20 overview](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/zone=3429/ruins-of-ahnqiraj), [BWL overview](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/blackwing-lair-raid-overview-classic-wow))

Mount gates need **purchase versus use** separated. Level/riding requirements on a mount item are not proof that buying the item requires those milestones. Native race OR Exalted is a potential eligibility alternative, and hidden/skipped native-race reputation rows must not block progress. The guide's savings estimate is never a hard purchase prerequisite: training spends that money. Classic racial-riding exceptions differ from the current faction-wide Forever behavior, so avoid copying them into Forever locks. Skyborne vendor/trainer eligibility, PvP mount/weapon ranks, tabard purchase restrictions and the Scarab Gong event remain current-build verification tasks. ([Classic riding guide](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/wow-classic-mounts-riding-skill), [Classic Swift Yellow Mechanostrider](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=18774/swift-yellow-mechanostrider), [Forever Galestrider item](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=269671/swift-empyrean-galestrider), [Galestrider passive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1285849/galestrider-riding), [Bang a Gong!](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=8743/bang-a-gong))

## Guide corrections come before production dependency edges

The Dreadsteed guide includes **Kroshius' Infernal Core, an unrelated Inferno spell quest**, and omits real ritual branches. Imp Delivery depends on Wildeyes and Xorothian Stardust; Arcanite follows the Bell/Wheel/Candle material handoffs; those two branches then converge on Dreadsteed of Xoroth. Another warlock can supply reusable ritual tools. Do not link Kroshius into this chain or declare the current short guide sufficient for hard locks. ([Kroshius' Infernal Core](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7603), [Imp Delivery](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7629), [Arcanite](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7630), [Dreadsteed of Xoroth](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7631))

The Charger guide has a **started-quest versus completed-quest trap**: Manna-Enriched Horse Feed requires Ancient Equine Spirit in progress, while completing Ancient Equine Spirit requires the feed. Current row7 says complete the Spirit quest before row9's feed. Requiring completed row7 to unlock row9 would create a false cycle. Use quest7643 active state, correct the presentation order or add an acceptance action with a saved-progress remap. Quest7641's relationship with Exorcising Terrordale disagrees between the pinned database and historical commentary; that edge remains withheld until direct verification. ([Ancient Equine Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7643), [Manna-Enriched Horse Feed](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7645), [The Work of Grimand Elmore](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7641))

Atiesh row8 says complete purification before row9's entity kill, even though the kill and return belong to that purification quest. Thunderfury row11 currently detects the summoning turn-in as proof of defeating Thunderaan. Rhok'delar's sinew turn-in text names Vartrus instead of Stoma. Quel'Serrar's blade-acquisition row has an early questTaken detector; its timed heated-blade state also cannot be proved by an old tick alone. These are data/evidence corrections, separate from lock animation. Removing/reordering saved positional steps requires a one-time remap. ([Atiesh purification](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=9257), [Rise, Thunderfury!](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7787), [A Proper String](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7635), [The Forging of Quel'Serrar](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=7509), [local auto rules](../Data.lua))

Tier 3 omits **Echoes of War9033**, which opens its Classic armor turn-ins after attunement. Its 285 existing material tasks remain available for gathering; the missing quest applies to a future turn-in action rather than every material row. The eight-piece-per-class goal does not currently contain a ring, so external nine-piece descriptions must not silently add one. Frostsaber's current level40/daily wording conflicts with the Classic baseline of level58 repeatables; verify Forever before changing the approved guide. ([Echoes of War](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=9033/echoes-of-war), [Tier 3 armor guide](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/naxxramas-tier-3-armor-set-wow-classic), [Frostsaber Provisions](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=4970))

**The incoming Forever build is explicitly unverified.** New raid entry rules, set-piece sources, vendor rank/riding behavior, quest changes and event availability can differ from Classic. The current Forever raid overview does not establish all encounter-access or set-source conditions. Preserve the existing Forever presentation and known behavior while checking that build; uncertain cases should show guidance rather than block legitimate manual progress. ([Forever raid overview](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/raids-overview-hub-dates-locations))

## Conclusion

The first implementation should pair a small, corrected quest-chain prototype with character-aware evidence. Real prerequisites generally mean **one eligible character satisfies all conditions**, not that separate alts contributed unrelated ticks. Cross-goal requirements should query game/roster evidence regardless of whether the source goal is tracked; missing character provenance or shared-instance proof prevents a reliable hard block. Prototype Benediction's linear/event-plus-independent-material structure, then use Lok'delar or Horde Onyxia to prove multi-step unlocks, and Thunderfury to prove OR logic. Keep the remaining candidates as guidance until their evidence and current client behavior are established.

The agreed visual first pass is a lock in place of the checkbox, a short sideways jiggle on a blocked click **without red tint on the lock**, muted red prerequisite explanation in the tooltip, and a break-away unlock revealing the checkbox. One completion can release multiple rows, with restrained stagger if useful. Preserve link interactions and animation settings. **Auto-detected completion overrides locks; completed steps never automatically untick or relock.** Saved positional rows stay stable or receive explicit remaps. The report, ledgers and shared handoff document the research only; Lua, goal data and the addon load list were not changed.
