# Player wording pass

Karl's direction: SIMPLE, HELPFUL, CLEAR. Players should focus on playing.

Reviewed steps, descriptions, tips and shared tooltips across Data.lua,
Library.lua, AQArmor.lua, Core.lua, Dependencies.lua and ItemTooltips.lua.
Already clear instructions remain. The retained before/after replacement
list is copy-edits-2026-10-12.json; it covers the main edits, with additional
scope formatting, short Forever tooltip variants and moved source tips in
the Git diff.

Examples:

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| Complete these prerequisites first: | Complete first: |
| Complete either prerequisite: | Complete one: |
| None of your characters have started it yet. | Not started on tracked characters. |
| Shift-click to put its name in chat. | Shift-click: name in chat |
| You can still click to tick it by hand. | Click to check off manually. |
| Have 25 friends on your friends list. | Have 25 friends. |

Goal descriptions lose filler and internal provenance. Tips keep practical
advice and important counts, timing, restrictions and uncertainty. A few
step parentheticals about rare drops and tradable materials now live in
linked tips. Shared armor steps begin with Collect; rank sources use at.
Exact item names, quoted quest names and existing item/goal links remain.

Shared click hints use action: result. The tracking explanation uses
Completes when. Race/faction/class scope appears after the conditions,
instead of incorrectly being described as another OR completion condition.
Forever tooltip notices are now short; goal pages retain the Classic
baseline explanation. Source unknown labels remain unchanged; this pass
does not introduce a new per-step uncertainty feature.

Persistent copy guidelines are recorded in AGENTS.md.
