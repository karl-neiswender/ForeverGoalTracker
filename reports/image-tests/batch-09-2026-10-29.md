# Batch 9 review images, 2026-10-29

Eight banners were generated with built-in imagegen from Karl's locked picks. The six trading card images A076-A081 were cropped to their painting windows first; the crop files are in `Media/final-images/source-crops`. The originals in `Media/artwork` remain untouched. Each generated 1448 x 1086 draft was resized to 1200 x 900 without other pixel edits. All eight reviews passed `tools/art-plan.py check-master` for dimensions, neutral grayscale, and baked left and bottom gradients. Karl approved all eight reviews shown on the Batch 9 sheet. The approved images were copied byte-for-byte to masters, encoded as 512 px BLP2/DXT1 textures with ten mip levels, and mapped by class, race, faction and sex. Native appearance needs a full WoW restart because the textures are new.
