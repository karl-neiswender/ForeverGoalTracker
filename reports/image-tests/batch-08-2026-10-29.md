# Batch 8 review images, 2026-10-29

Eight Orc class banners were generated with built-in imagegen from Karl's locked picks. Each generated 1448 x 1086 image was resized to 1200 x 900 without other pixel edits. All eight selected reviews passed `art-plan.py check-master` for size, neutral grayscale, and baked left and bottom gradients. A070 received a second draft to make the female Orc warlock read more clearly; both drafts are retained.

Karl approved all eight images shown on the review sheet, so A070 uses v2 and the other seven use v1. The approved reviews were copied without pixel changes to the masters, encoded as 512 px BLP2/DXT1 textures with ten mip levels, and mapped by class, race, faction and sex. Native appearance needs a full WoW restart because the textures are new. The A069 and A073 source originals were previously untracked and are included in this batch commit without modification.

## Exact initial imagegen prompts

For each of A066 through A073, the exact prompt was the following common text followed by one space and the row's `Source details` text below:

> Create one 4:3 landscape goal banner from the provided source artwork. Preserve the depicted character's race, sex, class, face, clothing or armor, hands, weapon or magical effects, and pose. Recompose the main character high and toward the right with the left third quiet for addon text. Match the source's Warcraft hand-painted fantasy illustration style with bold brushwork and stylized game-art anatomy, not photorealism. Strictly neutral black and white grayscale with moderate contrast, detailed painterly midtones and controlled highlights. Bake a wide smooth dark neutral-charcoal gradient into the LEFT side, strongest at the edge and fading naturally into the scene. Bake a soft dark neutral-charcoal gradient over roughly the lowest 100 pixels, fading to near-black at the bottom edge. Full-bleed painting with no hard gradient boundaries, added words, logos, border, artist signature, or watermark. No extra characters or unrelated objects. Source details:
