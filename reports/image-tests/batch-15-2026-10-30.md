## Generation instructions

Each initial imagegen request used its listed input to make a 4:3 Warcraft painterly fantasy banner, faithful to the source character and pose, in strictly neutral black and white. Each specified a quiet dark left third for addon text, a smooth baked charcoal gradient from the left edge, a soft dark gradient across the bottom, no card border or printed text, and no extra characters or unrelated props. The subject details were:

- A125: male blue-skinned Troll mage in dungeon robes, crouched with a short wand and violet spell burst.
- A127: male Dwarf priest with red beard and ornate cleric robes, confronting a burning undead skeleton in a stone chamber.
- A132: female Gnome warlock in a hood and skull shoulder guards, fists raised with magical flame before a stone arch.
- A133: male Orc warlock seated cross legged, holding a continuous diagonal jeweled staff with both hands, background staffs and a skull by his right knee.
- A134: male Dwarf warrior with a round shield and heavy hammer amid fiery debris, with the source signature removed.
- A136: female Night Elf druid, face raised, antler adornments and open casting hands with swirling leaves, without a staff.

A133 v2 was generated from the annotated v1 to straighten the staff in the Orc's hands and add the separate staff lying on the ground with the skull as its head. A133 v3 was generated from annotated v2 and Karl's close-up of the original source. The final instruction was to make the ground staff thinner and smoother with a few rings and a small forked tip at the left, preserving the rest of the scene. Karl approved v3.

All selected reviews passed `tools/art-plan.py check-master`.
