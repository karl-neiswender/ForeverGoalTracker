## A114 revisions

The initial A114 review failed the bottom-edge darkness check. A v2 edit darkened the lowest roughly 100 pixels while preserving the rest of the banner; it passed the technical check. Karl then requested a composition closer to the original source. The selected v3 was generated directly from the original painting with this prompt:

> Edit Image 1 into a 4:3 landscape Forever Goal Tracker banner. Treat the original as a locked composition. Keep the female gnome mage on the LEFT, at about the same relative size and pose as the source: head near the left-center, both casting hands projecting toward the viewer, short full body and feet visible in the lower-left. Keep the huge ogre on the RIGHT, his looming face and raised hand in the upper-right, his arm, torso, and leg occupying the right side. Maintain their confrontation, spacing, perspective, face shapes, costume shapes, and positions as closely as possible. The current request specifically requires a composition closer to the original source; do not move the gnome to the center or change the ogre to a small distant opponent. Preserve the same ice shards and environment. Render in strictly neutral black-and-white grayscale, with a flatter painterly Warcraft illustration style and controlled highlights. Integrate a smooth dark neutral-charcoal gradient into the leftmost area, strongest at the edge but preserving the gnome, and a smooth dark gradient over the lowest roughly 100 pixels fading to near-black at the bottom edge. No hard gradient boundary, color, text, border, logo, watermark, added characters, or unrelated changes.

The v3 check reported size `(1200, 900)`, left `24.3`, bottom `7.1`, mid `126.5`, and color deviation `1.1`. Karl then requested shifting the whole scene right to restore the banner's quiet left text area. The selected v4 uses v3 as Image 1 with this prompt:

Keep the gnome on the LEFT of the ogre, with the same relative scale, poses, facing directions, ice attack, and close confrontation as Image 1. Extend the rocky, icy background into the newly opened left third and make that left third quiet and dark enough for text with a smooth neutral-charcoal gradient. Keep both casting hands and the gnome's feet visible. Keep the ogre's face, raised hand, and large right-side torso visible even if the outermost ice shards crop at the edge. Retain the source-faithful composition within the shifted scene. Preserve strictly neutral black-and-white grayscale, the painterly Warcraft style, the bottom 100-pixel dark gradient fading to near-black, and 4:3 framing. No text, border, logo, color, extra figures, or unrelated changes.
