# Batch 7 review images, 2026-10-29

Eight banners were generated with built-in imagegen from Karl's locked source picks. The generated 1448 x 1086 images were resized to 1200 x 900 without other pixel edits. The selected eight reviews passed `art-plan.py check-master` for dimensions, neutral grayscale, and baked left and bottom gradients. A058 and A063 were revised after Karl requested a flatter Warcraft painterly style. Both drafts are retained; the review sheet showed v2 for each.

Karl approved all eight and explicitly selected **A058 v1** and **A063 v2**. These versions were copied without pixel changes to the masters. All eight are installed as 512 px BLP2/DXT1 textures with ten mip levels, mapped by class, race, faction and sex, and included in the checklist. The other six use v1. Native appearance requires a full game restart because these textures are new.
