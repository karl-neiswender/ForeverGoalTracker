# Batch 10 review images, 2026-10-29

Seven banners were generated with built-in imagegen from Karl's locked picks. A084 is blocked because its art is already installed and mapped, so no image was made for it. The trading card images A083, A085 and A087 were cropped to their painting windows first; those inputs are in `Media/final-images/source-crops`. Originals in `Media/artwork` remain untouched. Each generated 1448 x 1086 draft was resized to 1200 x 900 without other pixel edits. The selected seven reviews passed `tools/art-plan.py check-master`. Karl approved all seven, choosing A083 v3 with only the eyepatch band corrected and A086 v2 with the shield framing corrected. All drafts are retained. The approved reviews were copied byte-for-byte to the masters and encoded as 512 px BLP2/DXT1 textures with ten mip levels. A091 is one image installed for both female Undead Warrior and Paladin slots. Native appearance needs a full WoW restart because the textures are new.
