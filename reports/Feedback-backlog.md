# Player feedback backlog

Goal requests and ideas from the in-game Send feedback window (Google Form, emailed
to Gmail as "Forever Goal Tracker feedback"). The daily feedback check adds entries;
Karl and Claude decide in brainstorm sessions what gets built. Nothing here is
approved work until Karl says so.

Bugs are not kept here: each one goes to `reports/feedback/` with a diagnosis.

Format: one entry per request, duplicates merged with a count.

```
- **Black War Tiger goal** (asked 1 time; first 2026-11-03)
  Goal request. Claude: easy, fits the mounts group; needs its vendor and rep.
```
