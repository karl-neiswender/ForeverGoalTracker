# Next release, draft notes (not released; no version is authorized)

Written in the proposed style (`style-guide.md`) from everything on `main` since 2.12.0. Only things a player of 2.12.0 would notice are listed (bugs that existed only in unreleased builds are left out). Edit freely; the Developer's note is a placeholder for Karl's own words.

**Goal images come alive, and armor sets try on in one click.**

## New

- Added light to goal images. Embers drift off the fires, snow falls on the frozen banners, sunlight slants through the trees, and lightning gives off a soft glow. It all moves slowly, and nothing flashes.
- You can now Ctrl-click an armor set's icon, on its goal page or its Library card, to try on every piece in the game's dressing room.
- Added an Image effects choice under Settings > Images: Full, Subtle (just the drifting clouds) or Off (the image alone).
- Added a step-by-step checklist to every Tier 2.5 armor piece: its token, two idols and two scarabs, the same way Tier 3 lists them.
- Added a "Learn Fishing" first step to the Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza.

## Changes

- Goal suggestions now follow your professions. The Fishing Extravaganza appears only for characters with Fishing 225 or higher (was every character), and the secondary skills goal lists only the skills you know.
- Reputations, Ambassador goals, battleground reputations and the profession goals no longer say "Unconfirmed in Forever". Their steps are standings and skill levels the game reports itself.
- Tips now glide open and closed, and the shade at the bottom of a list eases out instead of popping off.
- Renamed "WoW Forever" to "Forever" and "Classic" to "Classic Era" in tips and tooltips, so each client has one name.
- Renamed the Social interest in the welcome window to "Socializing".
- Gold amounts in tips now read "1,200 gold" (was "1,200g").

## Fixes

- Fixed the tracker doing background work while it was closed (the gold shine on goal links kept scanning for text no one could see).
