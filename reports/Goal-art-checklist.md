# Goal art checklist: faction and class

Last updated 2026-10-25. Built from the banner resolver in `Core.lua` and the
[October 15 coverage report](banner-image-gaps-2026-10-15.md). Update a cell whenever
you find or approve an image: change its colour square and the "Found" note.

| Square | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 | **Needed.** No image for this combo. | Find one |
| 🟡 | **Borrowed.** Using another goal's or the other faction's image. | Find a proper one |
| 🟦 | **Shared.** One image serves both factions and looks fine. | Optional upgrade |
| 🟢 | **Done.** Image made for this faction and class. | Nothing |
| ⚪ | Not applicable | Nothing |
